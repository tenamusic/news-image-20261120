Entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261120 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) — "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 front-page infographic
summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in 28 sourced stories: a lead on the
first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen ending with no
breakthrough, a markets strip (Bitcoin under $62,000, S&P 500 −2.0%, gold
$3,608, Fed December hike odds near 90%), and 24 short stories across three
columns — Putin ruling out a sea/energy truce after Ukraine's third Crimea
Bridge strike of the week, three tankers ablaze near Hormuz, a Houthi strike
on Riyadh's tallest tower, the World Court's Myanmar genocide ruling, the
Senate vote to release the Epstein files, the Anthropic IPO debut, and more —
plus an "also today" line (Jeffries, Cyclone Akvan, GTA 6 in India), each with
its source named — to the output directory.

The image was drawn with a fresh Python/PIL script (`render20.py` in the
parent directory) from the day's reporting.
